What closes a trip

Three steps close every trip.

Each one is a line on your trip, with its time.

  1. Step 1ArrivedThe truck is at your site.
  2. Step 2DeliveredYour receiver signs the proof of delivery.
  3. Step 3OffloadedThe container is empty and ready to go back.

The document

Who signed, when, and for which container.

Every proof of delivery shows the waybill, the container and its seal on arrival, the truck and driver, who received it, when, and where it was signed. Open it beside the trip, or download the PDF.

Most are signed on paper today. The signed waybill stays with the record, one tab away.
